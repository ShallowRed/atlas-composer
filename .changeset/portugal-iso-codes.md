The atlases `portugal` and `europe` name the Azores `PT-20` and Madeira `PT-30`, as ISO 3166-2 does, and the presets of Portugal follow. A composite exported before this version names Madeira `PT-20` and the Azores `PT-30`: swap the two codes in the file to import it. A shared link of the app created before this version applies the scale and the offset of Madeira to the Azores.
