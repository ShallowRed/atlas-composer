The `europe` atlas has the territories Bonaire `NL-BQ1`, Saba `NL-BQ2` and Sint Eustatius `NL-BQ3`, which `buildTerritories` extracts from the Netherlands, in the collection `europe-overseas`, and the territories Svalbard `NO-21` and Jan Mayen `NO-22`, which `buildTerritories` extracts from Norway, in the collection `continental-europe` of the geographic region. The territory `NL` holds the European part of the Netherlands, and the territory `NO` the mainland of Norway.
